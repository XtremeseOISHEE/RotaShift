Local constraints

CONTEXT · PLACEHOLDER

Forecast

No live or seasonal forecast is connected in this prototype. Everything shown is derived from the historical record, never from a prediction of next season.

NOT CONNECTED

Simulation

The adaptation margin is an illustrative crop-window stress test. A planning aid, not an outcome prediction — it says when a plan stops fitting, not what it will yield.
